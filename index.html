Our Beginning

A tailoring table, before it was a brand.

Aishwarya Fashion started as a single tailoring table in Surat — a city that has quietly clothed half the country's weddings without ever putting its name on the label. What began as fittings for family and neighbours became a full atelier, built on the same principle: a garment should be discussed, not just bought.

We kept that habit even as we grew. There's still no checkout button on this site — because the right blouse depth, the right border weight, the right red, is still a conversation, not a dropdown.

Our Craft

Slow by design, not by delay.

Every piece is cut only after an enquiry — nothing sits pre-made on a shelf. Zardozi, mukaish, gota patti and hand-embroidery are finished by karigars we've worked with for years, most of them within driving distance of the atelier.

It means production takes longer than a factory line. It also means the stitching is checked by a person, not a machine — and it shows in how the piece drapes after the first wash.

What We Hold To

Three things we won't compromise.

Honest fabric

If we say silk, it's silk. We'll always tell you the blend before you commit, not after.

Realistic timelines

We'll tell you if your date is too tight for hand-embroidery — and suggest what will actually be ready in time.

One point of contact

From first message to final fitting, you speak to the same person on WhatsApp — no call centre hand-offs.

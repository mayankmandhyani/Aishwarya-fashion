    Our Beginning

    A tailoring table, before it was a brand.

    Surat has quietly clothed half the country's weddings without ever putting its name on the label. Aishwarya Fashion started as a single fitting table here — what began as work for family and neighbours became a full atelier, built on the same principle: a garment should be discussed, not just bought.

    Our Craft

    Slow by design, not by delay.

    Every piece is cut only after an enquiry — nothing sits pre-made on a shelf. Zardozi, mukaish, gota patti and hand-embroidery are finished by karigars we've worked with for years, most within driving distance of the atelier. It takes longer than a factory line — and it shows in how the piece drapes after the first wash.

    What We Hold To

    Three things we won't compromise.

    I

    Honest fabric

    If we say silk, it's silk. We'll tell you the blend before you commit, not after.

    II

    Realistic timelines

    We'll tell you if your date is too tight for hand-embroidery — and what will actually be ready in time.

    III

    One point of contact

    From first message to final fitting, you speak to the same person on WhatsApp.
